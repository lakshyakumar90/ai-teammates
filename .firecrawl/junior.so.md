## Frequently asked questions about AI employees.

How is an AI employee different from ChatGPT or Claude?+

ChatGPT is a tool you use. Junior is a teammate who uses tools. It has persistent memory of your organization, takes initiative on tasks, joins your workspace (Slack or Teams), reads meeting recaps and transcripts when you share them, and runs scheduled work 24/7 without being prompted.

What tools does my AI employee connect with?+

Slack or Microsoft Teams as the primary workspace, plus Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, Google Calendar, GitHub, Stripe, and 3,000+ more via Pipedream.

How long does it take to onboard an AI employee?+

Junior starts working the same day. Connect Slack or Teams plus email, and your AI employee begins delivering within hours.

Do we need Slack or Microsoft Teams?+

Yes, one of Slack or Microsoft Teams. That's where your AI employee shows up as a teammate, gets @mentioned, and joins threads alongside your team.

Is my data safe?+

Each AI employee runs in its own isolated runtime environment. Encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Never used for model training. Full audit logs.

What about prompt injection?+

Three layers: OAuth scopes that bound what tools Junior can call, model-side injection resistance, and our own input filters. Junior is designed not to act outside its authorized scope, and approval gates catch anything that would. We treat prompt injection as an ongoing arms race, not a solved problem.

Can I control what Junior accesses?+

Yes. Junior only accesses what you explicitly grant. Every action is logged and reviewable.

Does an AI employee replace my team?+

No. AI employees handle execution so your team focuses on strategy and relationships.

What if Junior gets something wrong?+

Junior's responses, summaries, and reports are AI-generated, and AI output can occasionally be inaccurate or incomplete. Review anything important before relying on it. When something is wrong, correct it directly in Slack and Junior records the correction for next time. Approval gates on customer-facing actions are on by default, so most mistakes get caught before they ship. Every action is logged with the agent's reasoning, including the ones you cancel.
